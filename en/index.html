How this site works

Read, listen, look up

School of Rock tells the history of popular music in thirteen mixtapes. Each mixtape is a chapter about a period or a theme: from blues, ragtime and rock 'n' roll via The Beatles, jazz, soul, punk and hip-hop to electronic music, Dutch pop and today's playlist. You read how genres grew out of one another, who made the music and what was happening in the world when it appeared.

Every listening tip comes with a fragment you can play, and every mixtape has its own Spotify playlist. On the genre map you see the whole history of pop as a family tree: 218 genres along the timeline and on the world map, with the influences between them. The site accompanies the pop history course at ArtEZ University of the Arts, but it is for anyone who wants to know where today's music comes from.

The genre map and HitStory, the pop encyclopedia, are available in English. The mixtape chapters are still in Dutch, so those links are marked NL; a translation is on the way.
